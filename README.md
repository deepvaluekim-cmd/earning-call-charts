# Earnings call chart assets

Public PNG assets used by the HPE, SMCI, and Lenovo Notion earnings-call pages.

Sources are the companies' official FY2026 earnings presentations. The images are selected full-slide renders with no editorial alteration.

## Asset inventory

- HPE FY2Q26: p.5 segment results, p.6 AI orders/backlog, p.14 Cloud & AI mix
- SMCI FY3Q26: p.4 business evolution, p.6 DCBBS, p.9 financial highlights
- Lenovo FY4Q26: p.9 group segments, p.20 ISG inflection, p.21 AI infrastructure

All published images are 1600x900 PNG files and are consumed by Notion through jsDelivr URLs pinned to the `main` branch.
